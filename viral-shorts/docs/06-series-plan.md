# Series Plan — the next 6 Shorts (same format, same pipeline)

The pipeline in `build/` is topic-agnostic: edit `LINES`, `SHOTS`, `CARDS`, `CUES` in
`build/video_build.py`, drop new images into `assets/img/`, and everything else (timing,
captions, SFX placement, loop) rebuilds itself. Post 1 Short/day for 3 weeks — daily posting
gets 3–5× more algorithmic distribution than weekly.

Every script below follows the same skeleton:
**impossible-sounding claim → number → twist → number → wrong-but-debatable line → loop.**

---

## Short 2 — "China built a power plant in the sky" (2–4 days later)
Hook: `A Chinese power plant is floating over your house right now.`
Beats: orbital solar array → beaming power to Earth with microwaves → 3.6 million km² of panels needed → "it can't fry a bird, right?" → loop on the array unfolding.
Comment bait: "Microwave beams can't hurt birds." (People will correct — the beam density question is genuinely debated.)

## Short 3 — "This robot learned to walk… after it died" (day 3)
Hook: `This robot learned to walk after it was switched off.`
Beats: humanoid robots learning in simulation → 10,000 robot-years per night → sim-to-real gap → "waking up" moment → loop on the robot's first step.
Comment bait: "Robots can think even when they're off." (They can't — but the sim/deploy split is exactly the argument people will have.)

## Short 4 — "The AI that reads your X-rays missed this" (day 4)
Hook: `An AI read 10 million X-rays. It missed the ones that mattered.`
Beats: FDA-cleared imaging models → 94% accuracy → the 6% it misses are the rare cancers → one wrong AI answer cascades → loop.
Comment bait: "The AI is more accurate than a human doctor, always." (Deliberately absolute — radiologists will answer.)
⚠️ Health topic: keep it informational, no medical advice.

## Short 5 — "Your phone's battery has a hidden life story" (day 5)
Hook: `Your phone lies to you about 1%.`
Beats: battery calibration → voltage curve → 500 charge cycles → why 100% isn't full → loop on the battery icon.
Comment bait: "Charging overnight destroys your battery." (Partially true, mostly outdated — huge comment volume.)

## Short 6 — "The chip that costs more than a house" (day 6)
Hook: `This chip costs more than your house.`
Beats: AI accelerators → $40,000 per chip → 100,000 chips per data center → the water it drinks per day → loop.
Comment bait: "Data centers use as much water as a small city." (Depends how you count — instant correction war.)

## Short 7 — "The 'human' you argued with online" (day 7)
Hook: `You argued with a machine yesterday and never knew.`
Beats: agentic AI posting online → 100+ organizations already breached by rogue agents (Oct 2026 news) → AI-written replies passing as human → loop.
Comment bait: "AI can't tell if it's talking to another AI." (Contested; great bait.)

## Weekly cadence template
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Mon | Post Short (6–9 AM EST). Reply to every comment for 30 min |
| Tue | Post Short; boost the previous one with a pinned question |
| Wed | Reply-to-comments video idea check in Studio (retention graph) |
| Thu | Post Short; A/B test the title format |
| Fri | Post Short; start cutting a long-form video from the best-performing topic |
| Sat/Sun | Rest or repost the best Short to Reels/TikTok |

## Reusable hook formulas (swap the nouns and they work in any niche)
1. "A [company] [thing] is [doing something] right now."
2. "This [cheap object] costs more than [expensive object]."
3. "It can only [do the core job] for [shockingly short time]."
4. "You [did an everyday thing] yesterday and never knew."
5. "[Big number] [things] are [doing something] above your head right now."

## What to feed back into the next script
- Retention graph from Studio: any dip > 3% at one second = cut that beat from the next script.
- Comment keyword mining: whatever people correct most becomes next week's deliberate error (rule #6 compounds).
- Titles that won: reuse the exact sentence shape, not just the topic.
